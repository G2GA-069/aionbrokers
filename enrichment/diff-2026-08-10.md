**Rotation parameters:**
- Date: 2026-08-10 (ISO week 33)
- `(week_of_year - 1) mod 8 = (33 - 1) mod 8 = 0`
- All firms where `array_index mod 8 == 0` (51 firms total), sorted by name, first 19 taken
- Plus 6 firms from the lowest-confidence half (deterministic step-sampling)

**Total WebFetch calls:** 0 direct (all blocked by egress proxy)
**WebSearch calls:** ~20 (used as fallback for all website health checks and team research)

**Egress proxy:** Direct WebFetch to all external firm domains returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`. All Phase 1 health checks and Phase 2 team research were conducted via WebSearch (Google index), which introduces a lag — pages that have been de-indexed or are very new may not appear. Team-page data in Phase 2 is sourced from search snippets and third-party aggregators (Mergr, PitchBook, RocketReach, Tracxn), not direct team-page reads.

**JavaScript-rendered pages:** Not applicable (no direct fetches attempted).

**Firms with no website in BD (4):** GB-047 Bluebox, AXIAL-0626 Calash, AXIAL-0627 Convex Capital, AXIAL-0629 Langcliffe — all confirmed active and websites found via search.
