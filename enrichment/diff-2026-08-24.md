**Egress-proxy limitation (dominant issue this run).**
`WebFetch` returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` for every firm domain tried:
- `www.acuityadvisors.com`, `albertgoodman.co.uk`,
  `www.armstrongwatson.co.uk`, `www.asgcommercial.co.uk`,
  `www.investmentbank.barclays.com` — and separately

  transient / IPv6 issue rather than a dead firm).
- Even `companieshouse.gov.uk` was policy-blocked, so the standard fallback
  (fetch Companies House pages for confirmations) is also unavailable.
Effect: **the entire homepage-liveness check that Phase 1 is designed
around cannot run**. The workflow was adapted to WebSearch-only signal for
every phase. That's meaningfully weaker: closures that Google still surfaces
recent hits for won't be caught, and team pages can't be extracted. Once the
egress policy is amended (allow-listing `find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk`
plus a general "reasonable UK M&A firm websites" list, or otherwise
un-blocking outbound HTTPS for this session), a follow-up pass can re-run
the sample properly.
